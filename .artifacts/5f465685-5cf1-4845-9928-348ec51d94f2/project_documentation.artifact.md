# CampusConnect – Project Comprehensive Documentation

**CampusConnect** is a next-generation social ecosystem designed specifically for the modern university campus. It bridges the gap between academic formalness and spontaneous social interaction, transforming how students discover, coordinate, and engage with their community.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Unified Event Discovery
- **Live Feed**: A real-time, aesthetic feed of all campus happenings.
- **Advanced Search**: Instant filtering by title or description to find exactly what you're looking for.
- **Smart RSVP**: One-tap attendance tracking with automated **Waitlist Logic** that triggers once an event reaches capacity.

### 2. Specialized Study Groups
- **Filtered View**: A dedicated section for academic coordination.
- **Direct Integration**: Join groups for specific subjects (like DBMS or UI/UX) and see schedules at a glance.

### 3. Advanced Logistics (The "Pro" Features)
- **QR Ticket System**: Every attendee gets a unique, secure QR ticket.
- **Organizer Scanner**: Event hosts have a built-in scanner to check-in attendees, providing real attendance data.
- **Group Chat**: Localized, event-specific chat rooms for coordination ("Where are we meeting?", "Bring your laptop chargers!").

---

## 🌟 Innovation "Next-Gen" Features (The Wow Factor)

What makes CampusConnect unique compared to standard event apps:

- **Campus Karma (Reputation System)**: A peer-vouched trust system. Students earn Karma points for being helpful, which build community authority and trust.
- **Skill Swap Economy**: A non-monetary marketplace where students trade knowledge (e.g., "Math Tutoring for a Coffee"). It creates a "knowledge economy" on campus.
- **Real-time "Flash" Sessions**: Spontaneous, self-destructing study sessions that last only 1 hour. Perfect for immediate help in the library or quick coffee meetups.
- **Aesthetic Cartoon Avatars**: A personalization system with 36+ unique characters, allowing students to express themselves while maintaining a cohesive, high-end app aesthetic.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

- **Language**: 100% Kotlin (Modern Android Standard).
- **Architecture**: MVVM-ready with reactive Data Flows.
- **Database**: Room Persistence Library with 14 integrated data entities.
- **UI Toolkit**: Material 3 (Google's latest design system) with custom styling and 20dp rounded aesthetics.
- **Key Libraries**:
    - **Coil & Coil-SVG**: For high-performance, aesthetic image loading.
    - **ZXing**: For robust QR code generation and scanning.
    - **Navigation Component**: For seamless, type-safe screen transitions.

---

## 💎 Why is this useful for us? (Value Proposition)

### 1. Solves "Campus Fragmentation"
Information on campus is usually scattered across WhatsApp groups, emails, and notice boards. CampusConnect centralizes everything into one single, beautiful source of truth.

### 2. Encourages Spontaneous Collaboration
The **Flash Sessions** feature encourages students to help each other *right now*, reducing the friction of scheduling formal meetings.

### 3. Builds a Safer, Trusted Community
The **Campus Karma** system ensures that you know who the helpful and reliable members of the community are before you meet them or trade items with them.

### 4. Zero-Cost Knowledge Sharing
The **Skill Swap** allows students to get help with difficult subjects without needing money, making academic support accessible to everyone regardless of their budget.

### 5. High Practicality for Demonstrations
For a CA1 project, this app goes far beyond basic CRUD operations. It demonstrates expertise in **Camera/Hardware integration (QR)**, **Reactive Databases**, **Personalization**, and **Complex UI/UX Design**.
